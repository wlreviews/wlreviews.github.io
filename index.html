Weight-loss apps, reviewed the slow way.

We compare behaviour-change apps and meal plans on price, method, and fit — checking every figure against the official page before we print it. No promises about results, no before-and-after stories, no invented reviews.

What we do

  • Compare programs side by side, including a third option, so no review is a two-horse race.
  • Check price, trial terms, and features against official pages before publishing.
  • Say plainly who a program may suit — and who it may not.
  • Label anything unverified as “To be verified” instead of guessing.

What we don’t do

  • Promise pounds lost or a timeframe for results.
  • Publish testimonials or before-and-after stories.
  • Recommend weight-loss products to anyone under 18.
  • Write about GLP-1 medication programs or weight-loss patches.
