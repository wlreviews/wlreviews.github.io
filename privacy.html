Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026.

Honest Weight Loss Reviews is a small, independent review site for adults in the United States who are comparing paid weight-loss programs. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Contact us at piaccte@gmail.com about anything on this page.

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We do not run user accounts, and we do not ask for payment details or passwords. Our email newsletter is not yet running; this policy will be updated before it launches.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, we collect your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, the links you click, the site that referred you, and your approximate location. We use cookies for this. The analytics tool we use is Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-PH5RE99LD7).

How we use it

  • To run the site and fix problems.
  • To measure which pages and articles readers find useful.
  • To reply to your emails.
  • To prevent abuse and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and tracking

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell us how readers use the site. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's own opt-out add-on. Blocking cookies will not stop the site from working.

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy from the merchant, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The merchant may set its own cookies when you follow such a link, and those cookies are governed by the merchant's privacy policy, not this one. See our affiliate disclosure for the full explanation.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email piaccte@gmail.com and we will act on your request.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to ask us to delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

EU and UK residents. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, and restrict the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on it.

Children's privacy

This site is written for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Data retention

We keep emails you send us for as long as needed to answer them and to keep a record of corrections. Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics under its standard retention settings.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including limiting access to the site's analytics and email accounts. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

This site links to other websites, including the official pages of the programs we review and our Pinterest and YouTube channels. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. The current version is always the one on this page.
