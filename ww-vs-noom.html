WeightWatchers vs Noom: an honest comparison

Published 20 November 2026 as an outline with opening paragraphs. Full text to follow once the buyer persona and keyword checks are done.

The short answer: WeightWatchers and Noom are both behaviour-change programs, but they work differently. WeightWatchers is built around its points system and community; Noom is built around short psychology-based lessons and food colour categories. Which one fits you depends on whether you want group structure or daily lessons, and on price — and the current price and trial terms for both programs are still to be verified against their official pages before we publish a recommendation.

That is the honest state of this page today. It is an outline, not a finished review. Every price, trial term, and feature claim in the full text will be checked against the official pages on the day of writing, and anything that cannot be checked will stay labelled “To be verified”.

A third option runs through the whole comparison: MyFitnessPal. It does a different job — calorie tracking, mostly free — and for some readers it is the right answer, which is why a two-program comparison that never mentions it would be incomplete.
