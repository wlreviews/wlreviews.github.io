This page explains how Honest Weight Loss Reviews makes money, in line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255). The short version appears at the top of our articles and in the footer of every page; this page is the full version.

How affiliate links work

Some links on this site are affiliate links, also called referral links. If you click one and buy from the merchant, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. The link only tells the merchant where you came from. You pay the same price as anyone else.

Affiliate links go through a dedicated recommendation page — this site's comparison articles — and are never hidden in a footnote.

What commission does not change

  • Commission does not change what we write. Prices and trial terms are checked against the programs' official pages, with the check date printed beside each figure.
  • Every program's weak points are listed, including the weak points of whichever program we recommend.
  • If the checks do not support a clear recommendation, we say so rather than force one.
  • Rankings are not affected by commission. A merchant cannot pay for a place in a ranking, a higher score, or a positive review.
  • If we ever receive a free product to test, that does not guarantee a review, and it does not guarantee a positive one.

Sponsored content

We have no sponsored content. If that ever changes, any sponsored article will say so at the top of the article.

What we do not do

  • We do not promise pounds lost or a timeframe for results.
  • We do not publish testimonials, before-and-after stories, or reviews from people who did not use the product.
  • We do not publish AI-generated content presented as a real person's experience.
