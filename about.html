About and disclosure

Who we are

Honest Weight Loss Reviews is a small, independent review site for adults in the United States who are comparing paid weight-loss programs. We focus on behaviour-change apps and meal plans — starting with Noom and WeightWatchers — and we write in plain language for people who want the facts before they pay.

We are not a clinic, and this site is not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, talk to a clinician before starting a weight-loss program.

How we review

  • Three options, not two. Every comparison includes a third alternative, so no review is a two-horse race.
  • Same five points, every time. Price and trial terms, what you do each day, coaching and support, food tracking, and cancelling.
  • Figures checked, not remembered. Every price, trial term, and feature claim is checked against the program’s official pages on the day of writing, with the check date printed beside it. Anything unverified is labelled “To be verified” in place — never filled with a plausible number.
  • Real weaknesses listed. Every program’s weak points are printed, including the weak points of whichever program we recommend.
  • A second check before publishing. Facts, compliance, and originality are checked by someone other than the writer before anything goes live.
  • No star ratings. We do not give star scores. A comparison earns its recommendation in words, with reasons the reader can check.

Affiliate disclosure

Honest Weight Loss Reviews may earn a commission if you buy through a link on this site, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links go through a dedicated recommendation page — this site’s comparison articles — and are never hidden in a footnote.

Commission does not change what we write. Prices and trial terms are checked against official pages, weaknesses are listed, and if the checks do not support a clear recommendation, we say so rather than force one.

For adults only

This site is written for adults aged 18 and over. We do not promote weight-loss products to anyone under 18, and we do not write content aimed at minors.

What we don’t do

  • We do not promise pounds lost or a timeframe for results. We describe how programs work, not what they will do for you.
  • We do not publish testimonials, before-and-after stories, or reviews from people who did not use the product.
  • We do not cover GLP-1 medication programs, weight-loss patches, or supplements of unknown origin.
  • We do not publish AI-generated content presented as a real person’s experience.
